What is CPU scheduling?

A computer has many programs but few CPU cores. The scheduler decides which process runs next — and for how long.

Why it matters

A good algorithm keeps processes from waiting too long, makes the CPU busy, and treats every program fairly. Different workloads need different algorithms — that is why we compare them all.

ARRIVAL (AT)

When the process enters the queue.

BURST (BT)

CPU time the process needs.

TURNAROUND

CT − AT. Total time in system.

WAITING

TAT − BT. Time spent waiting.

The 6 algorithms

Each one makes a different trade-off between speed, fairness and simplicity.
